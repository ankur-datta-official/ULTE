Causal account and signal/portfolio separation
----------------------------------------------
CAUSAL_ACCOUNT_CONTEXT = YES for this research replay. Initial equity is
10,000 USDT once. Requested risk is 1% of then-current account equity.
At each closed 1h boundary, only earlier complete 15m bars and funding
events advance the account. Positions enter at the next 15m open. Open risk
comes from actual sized position risk; daily start equity resets at the UTC
day boundary after known events. Funding changes account cash only when a
held position spans an observed settlement. Closed price/fee PnL changes
equity only after the observed exit. Money arithmetic uses the shared exact
decimal package. Future PnL is never supplied to a decision. Account
snapshots at actionable boundaries are exported; an independent Python
fill/PnL model reconciles all four fills, quantities, equity steps, and
final equity. No candidate was blocked by the portfolio or sizing gates.
Signal-level and executable/portfolio results are consequently identical
for this dataset. There were no overlapping positions or unresolved trades.
This does not establish that the modeled account is a live account history.

Research limitations
--------------------
This is a candle-path fill model. Intra-15m stop/target ordering is unknown,
so a bar touching both stops first. Stops gap to the worse of bar open or
barrier; targets fill at barrier; entry/exit costs are fixed assumptions.
Funding uses entry notional because settlement mark prices were not in the
downloaded funding archive. Order-book queue, market impact and partial
fills are not modeled. The research config is test-derived and the prior
OOS is burned. No production equivalence, alpha certification, live-trading
readiness or pristine forward evidence is asserted.
