Current pipeline (source audit)
Normalized market trades -> shared market-data candle/MTF builder -> closed
as-of analysis frame -> regime classifier and confirmed structure/liquidity ->
three setup families -> deterministic decision gate -> structural risk/net RR
qualification -> portfolio eligibility -> sizing -> ready trade intent or
explicit no-decision/rejection. There is no score, confidence, probability,
learned prediction, or broker fill at this stage. The intent is not a trade.
The setup families are TREND_PULLBACK_CONTINUATION, BREAKOUT_RETEST, and
LIQUIDITY_SWEEP_REVERSAL. Entry reference is the confirmation close;
invalidation is the initiation sweep extreme or breakout retest extreme;
primary target is the nearest active eligible structural liquidity level.
The official qualification floor is 3.00 net RR under caller-supplied bps costs.

Data-integrity audit
Existing replay rejects backward timestamps; the live/replay candle engine
shares finalization, and analysis frames only include closed candles ending by
as-of. Structure pivots are published after right-window confirmation;
liquidity references and setup confirmations have as-of checks. Structural
risk rejects future setup candles/structure and enforces exact-decimal 3R net
qualification. These are source and regression-test findings, not proof of
real-data correctness. No random train/test split or training fit exists.
The new harness rejects duplicate/disordered/gapped/open candles, future MTF
frame candles, mismatched cycle/intent provenance, and below-floor signals.
It uses fixed chronological TRAIN/VALIDATION/TEST cutoffs and excludes trades
that cross a cutoff from segment metrics. No source-correctness rule in the
existing engines required a change in this task.

Backtest assumptions and limitations
The existing backtest engine replays events but has no fill or metrics engine.
The new research harness enters at the next candle open with configured
adverse spread/slippage, charges entry/exit fees and per-candle funding, uses
stop-first for ambiguous bars, and degrades stop gaps to the candle open.
Targets do not receive favorable gap improvement. It reports a trade ledger,
R curve, headline and split metrics, regime/setup buckets, no-trade counts,
frequency, exposure, and conservative MFE/MAE bounds. It does not model order
book liquidity or partial fills. Its cost settings must be checked against
the risk engine's cost assumptions before treating a 3R qualification as
economically comparable. It cannot authenticate an exported JSON result or
certify a data vendor; every run remains uncertified pending evidence review.
