Data quality and replay
The existing OHLCV importer accepted all four complete, strictly ordered,
unique, UTC-aligned kline series with valid OHLCV and zero missing intervals.
Official inclusive close timestamps equal open+duration-1ms; normalized
exclusive close/availability is open+duration. No candle is forward filled.
Funding has 7,395 ordered 8-hour settlements and zero interval gaps.
However, Binance's 15m OHLC does not aggregate to its own 1h OHLC at
2023-11-10T15:00:00Z and 2023-11-10T16:00:00Z. The 1h bars also disagree
with the 4h aggregate at 2023-11-10T12:00:00Z and 2024-10-28T20:00:00Z.
1h-to-1d and 4h-to-1d aggregates have zero mismatches. All four pairwise
exceptions are recorded in the manifest; the two 15m/1h exceptions are also
in the baseline result. None intersects a simulated trade; the fill harness
fails closed if an open trade intersects either 15m/1h mismatch hour. This is
an unresolved source-quality exception, not repaired or silently substituted.
The replay forwards only finalized 1h/4h/1d candles at their close boundaries
to the same shared ULTE analysis and decision engines used by application
logic. The 15m series is used only for fill ordering and MFE/MAE. A complete
59,160-cycle verification found no future or open frame candle. The generic
baseline harness separately validated paired cycle identities and boundaries.

Research configuration and costs
No production strategy thresholds or historical account snapshots exist in
the repository. At user direction, this exploratory run used the analysis
engine's documented test thresholds, the structure engine's 5-bar test
lookback with 1/1 pivots, and the setup engine's test regime allowlists.
Regime role was 4h, structure/setup role 1h; 1d was observed in the frame but
is not consumed by current strategy logic. A synthetic static 10,000 USDT
account, 100 USDT requested risk and 0.001 BTC quantity step supplied the
decision context. These assumptions cannot certify historical eligibility.
The ULTE 3.00 net projected reward/risk floor was unchanged. The base risk
cost assumption is 7 bps at entry and each exit. Fill costs are 5 bps taker
fee at entry and exit (Binance regular-user example:
https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/detail/360033544231), plus fixed
1 bp spread and 1 bp adverse slippage on each side. Historical spread and
slippage are not reconstructed. The worse case keeps fees at 5 bps but uses
2 bps spread and 2 bps slippage on each side, with unchanged signals.
Next-open entry, worse stop gaps, no favorable target gap improvement and
stop-first for unknown ordering within a 15m bar are deterministic. Actual
funding rates are applied on entry notional when a held interval includes a
settlement; settlement mark prices are unavailable, so entry notional is an
approximation. None of these four short-lived trades crosses a settlement.
