PHASE33C D5 final safety race audit

Baseline: main and origin/main were a497a69220373f2d205a75762394e32955862c60;
worktree clean. D4 boot is historical snapshot classification only and grants no
runtime execution permission. READY_ALLOWED remains NO.

Existing race coverage map (deterministic real PostgreSQL unless marked unit):
- Pending-intent commit: COVERED, B1F P1-P4 (duplicate, lost COMMIT, stale
  fence, final lease expiry) and X2-X3 (rollback and unique receipts).
- Outcome adoption: COVERED, B1F A1-A5 (duplicate/conflict, lost COMMIT,
  nested pending, final lease expiry).
- Adoption versus pending commit on one legal revision: COVERED, B1F X1;
  one lease-lock winner, loser REVISION_CONFLICT, one revision advance.
- Lease/fence takeover: COVERED, B1F L1-L3; old-owner fresh mutations fail.
- D3 terminal closure: COVERED, D3 concurrent duplicate/conflicting receipts,
  expiry, lost COMMIT, and terminal versus unlinked fill adoption.
- D3N normal closure: COVERED, D3N concurrent duplicate/conflicting receipts,
  expiry, stale fence, fenced status rejection, and legacy broker writer lock.
- D4 boot versus terminal commit: COVERED, D4 pinned pre-commit snapshot
  versus fresh terminal snapshot on distinct backends.
- Generic fenced writer: COVERED by the fenced-writer unit suite for terminal
  state, identity, revision, fence, and final lease rollback; D3N adds a real
  terminal broker writer race. No separate D5 writer race is justified.
- Idempotency claim/status versus boot: PARTIALLY_COVERED before D5 (pure
  classifier and writer guards, no coherent real boot interleaving); D5 closes it.
- Boot versus pending commit: MISSING before D5; D5 closes it.
- Boot versus outcome adoption: MISSING before D5; D5 closes it.
- Same-session concurrent boots: NOT REQUIRED: read-only snapshots cannot
  acquire, mutate, or grant executable authority.
- Boot versus lease takeover: NOT REQUIRED as an executable-authority race:
  boot is snapshot-only. Its classification is as-of its snapshot; fresh
  mutation still locks and validates the current lease/fence (B1F L3 and
  fenced-writer guards). A pre-takeover READY classification is not a permit.
- Lease expiry during authority transition: COVERED by B1F P4/A5, D3 and
  D3N final lease rollback, and fenced-writer final lease tests.

New D5 proofs (3 real PostgreSQL tests, four writer interleavings):
1. Legal pending commit from revision 1, writer held after receipt insert:
   pinned boot sees revision 1/checkpoint entry-before/no pending and READY
   as-of that committed snapshot. Fresh boot sees revision 2 plus pending,
   checkpoint and receipt and requires INTENT_DISPOSITION_REQUIRED. Durable
   revision/checkpoint/pending/receipt counts are 2/entry-after/1/1.
2. Legal unlinked fill adoption with nested protection pending, writer held
   after adoption receipt insert: pinned boot sees OUTCOME_AVAILABLE at
   revision 1 with no pending. Fresh boot sees revision 2 and the next pending,
   requiring INTENT_DISPOSITION_REQUIRED. Durable outcome/adoption receipt/
   pending counts are 1/1/1, checkpoint adopt-fill-next.
3. Legal pending entry effect, fenced claim and then fenced SUBMITTED update:
   pinned boots observe ABSENT then CLAIMED; fresh boots require
   INTENT_DISPOSITION_REQUIRED then RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED. Durable revision
   stays 2, one pending remains, broker status SUBMITTED. No blind retry.

Each D5 interleaving uses separate PostgreSQL backends, query-result barriers,
bounded deadlines, a REPEATABLE READ READ ONLY pinned snapshot opened before
the writer commits, and remaining authority reads after the writer commits.
The writer is held after its real SQL mutation. MVCC reads intentionally do
not block, so pg_blocking_pids is inapplicable; existing lock-contention
proofs in B1F/D3/D3N use it. Final durable rows are asserted.

Production defect found and fixed: after adoption created a next pending
effect, boot passed the entire effect to an exact-shape pending identity
validator and threw TypeError. Boot now passes only the six identity fields.
The D5 adoption race reproduced the defect before the fix and passes after it.
No schema or trading semantics changed.

READY safety: fresh boots return INTENT_DISPOSITION_REQUIRED,
OUTCOME_AVAILABLE, or RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED as their durable facts require;
the pure classifier unit suite covers CANONICAL_OUTCOME_REQUIRED,
RECOVERY_REJECTED, and conflicting evidence. D4 real tests cover terminal
non-submission and rejected corrupt terminal tuples. Historical READY in the
pre-commit pending snapshot grants no executable permission. READY_ALLOWED=NO.
Terminal safety: D3/D3N real closure tests and the commit/fenced-writer unit
guards reject new pending, adoption, status mutation, or revision advancement
after terminal closure. D4 boot returns TERMINAL_NON_SUBMISSION without
execution authority; the immutable terminal ref is retained. No D5 repeat.
CONFIRMED_REJECTED without canonical typed rejection remains blocked;
RECONCILIATION_CANONICAL_REJECTION_BLOCKER=OPEN.
